Vessa Partner Kit

Eren's field playbook — how to open, handle objections, and sign a location partner

00 — The core idea (read first)

You're not selling surgery. You're handing them free extra income.

Eren never sells to the customer. Eren sells to the owner/manager. The pitch is always the same: "I'm giving you a no-cost income channel from procedures you can't offer anyway."

Golden rule: Never say "I'll pay you per patient." That's a kickback framing. Say "marketing partnership" and "partner reward when a guest completes their trip." Same money, clean structure.
01 — The 30-second opener
"Hi — I run the US side of Vessa, we partner with accredited aesthetic clinics in Istanbul. Your clients trust you with how they look. When one of them wants something you don't do here — a hair transplant, a BBL, a facelift — right now you just lose that conversation. We turn it into income for you. You keep a brochure at reception, mention us when it fits, and when a guest actually travels and completes their journey, you earn a partner reward. Zero cost, zero effort, no risk to your own services. Can I show you how it works?"
Delivery: Warm, unhurried, peer-to-peer. Eren is a business partner, not a salesman. Leave the brochure + one-pager even if they say "let me think."
02 — Objection handling

The four things they'll say

Cannibalization
"Won't this take business away from me?"

"The opposite. We only handle surgical procedures you can't legally do here — I'm not touching your Botox, fillers or lasers. Today those surgical clients just walk out the door. This keeps them yours and pays you for it."

Trust / risk
"What if something goes wrong — it's my name on the line."

"Fair. That's why we only use accredited clinics with board-certified surgeons, and our own team meets every guest in Istanbul. There's a revision policy and 12 months of follow-up. You're introducing them to a real team, not shipping them off alone. If it ever went wrong, that's on us to handle — and we protect your reputation because it protects ours."

Effort
"I don't have time to sell surgery to people."

"You don't sell anything. The brochure sits at reception, the website does the explaining. You just mention it when it comes up naturally — 'I know a team in Istanbul, take a card.' That's it. We do the rest."

Money
"What's actually in it for me?"

"A partner reward for every guest who travels and completes their journey through your referral link. It scales — the more you mention us, the more you earn, with no cost and no downside. And it's paid only on completed trips, so it's real money, not promises."

03 — How the tracking works (explain simply)
  • Each partner gets a unique QR code / link on their brochure and reception card.
  • The guest scans it → lands on our page → messages us. That first contact ties them to your location automatically.
  • You get a monthly statement: leads sent, trips completed, reward earned.
  • Reward is paid after the guest completes their procedure and settles with the clinic — never on no-shows.
Why "after completion": protects everyone. No arguments over people who inquired but never traveled. Real trips = real rewards.
